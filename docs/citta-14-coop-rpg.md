# Città XIV — Lumequieto (Co-op RPG)

## Phase 1 — gameplay loop
- Party: 2–5 players, choose from the first 29 roster entries.
- Each player receives three actions per round.
- Actions: Explore, Talk, Attack, Protect, Switch, Train, Trade, and Recovery.
- Recruit one bench Sognatore per player; starter creatures remain protected from trade.
- Explore and encounter flags gate advancement through town, forest, archive, and boss.
- Encounter and boss HP are party-size scaled; the enemy retaliates after the team round.
- Protect reduces the next retaliation, Recovery restores HP, Training increases attack output, and Switch exchanges active/bench creature.
- Shared local UI state only. Online synchronization, persistence, dungeon map and integrated battle engine remain future phases.

## Story
1. Piazza delle Lanterne — obtain a clue.
2. Bosco dei Sussurri — explore and defeat the Custode delle Radici.
3. Archivio Sommerso — activate a rune and defeat the Sentinella.
4. Sala del Sigillo — defeat the scaled Sigillo Vivente.
